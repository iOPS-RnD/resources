== Graph DB and SPARQL

* https://lms-academy.ontotext.com/[Ontotext Academy]
** The academy is free, you just need to register with your email.
** Has two learning paths: "GraphDB Developer" and "GraphDB Knowledge Graph Engineer", each consisting of 3 courses
** The second learning path has 3 courses:
*** Course 1: Introduction to Semantic Technologies
*** Course 2: Semantic models with GraphDB
*** Course 3: SPARQL
** Normally the 3 courses need to be taken in sequence, and we highly recommend it if you have no experience with semantic technologies or want to get a certificate.
** To take only a selected course, register and then contact "bob dot ducharme at graphwise.ai"
* https://www.learningsparql.com/[Learning SPARQL]: a famous book by Bob that you can buy on https://www.amazon.com/Learning-SPARQL-Querying-Updating-1-1/dp/1449371434/[Amazon] in paper or Kindle format.
* https://sandbox.ontotext.com/[GraphDB Sandbox] - GraphDB ready-to-go and pre-packaged projects coupled with detailed walkthroughs
